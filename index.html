ShareFile Savings Calculator — Step-by-Step User Guide

For internal use only. Pricing sourced from public vendor pages as of May 2026. Subject to change.

1

Enter your customer's current tech stack

Go to the Sales Input tab.

  • Select the customer's current E-Sign Provider from the dropdown (e.g. DocuSign, Adobe Sign).
  • Select their current File Sharing Tool (e.g. M365 Business Standard, Dropbox Business Standard).
  • Enter the number of users.

Pricing Mode = 'Price Book (SRP)': prices auto-fill from verified Price Book.
Pricing Mode = 'Manual Entry': type the customer's actual prices in the orange cells (use their invoice for accuracy).

2

Select billing cycle

Choose how the customer currently pays:

  • Annual — pays yearly (lowest rate, most common).
  • Monthly — pay-as-you-go (highest rate).

All savings calculations normalize to an annual figure for consistent comparison.

3

Select the ShareFile plan to propose

Choose the ShareFile plan you are recommending:

  • Advanced — Encrypted storage, secure email, co-editing.
  • Premium — + Client portal, e-sign (100k docs/mo), AI workflows.
  • Industry Advantage — + Tax templates, bulk onboarding, accounting workflows.
  • Virtual Data Room — + Granular permissions, audit trails, watermarking.

ShareFile pricing auto-fills from the Price Book at the selected billing cycle.

4

Review competitive savings

The calculator shows:

  • Competitor Stack Total: current annual cost (E-Sign + Collaboration × users).
  • ShareFile Annual Cost: proposed plan × users at selected billing cycle.
  • Annual Savings $: Competitor Total − ShareFile Total.
  • Savings % (Cost Reduction): Annual Savings ÷ Competitor Total × 100.
  • 3-Year Savings $: Annual Savings × 3 (flat pricing assumed).

These figures are at ShareFile's STANDARD LIST PRICE — before any deal discount.

5

Enter your deal price (optional)

In the DEAL DISCOUNT SAVINGS section:

  • ShareFile SRP $/User/Yr auto-fills from the Price Book annual rate.
  • Enter your Deal Price $/User/Yr — the discounted price you are quoting.
  • Deal Discount %: (SRP − Deal Price) ÷ SRP × 100.
  • Deal Discount $/User/Yr: dollar value of your discount per user.
  • Total Effective Savings %: how much cheaper ShareFile is vs their current stack AT YOUR DEAL PRICE.
  • 3-Year Deal Savings $: total savings over 3 years at deal pricing.

Deal discounts are subject to ShareFile's discount authorization policy. This is for illustration purpose only - please refer the final quote for calculating discount.

6

Export to PowerPoint

Click the 'Export to Slides' button. The deck downloads through your browser with the name:
ShareFile Savings - [Company Name] - [Date].pptx

  • Slide 1: Cover / Disclaimer slide — Prepared for [Company Name].
  • Slide 2: Executive Summary — Competitor cost vs ShareFile cost vs Savings.
  • Slide 3: Cost Comparison Detail + Sales Talk Track.
  • Slide 4: Deal Discount Summary (if deal price entered).

The PowerPoint uses the Progress 2026 brand styling.
